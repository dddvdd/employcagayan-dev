# Proposed retention matrix — INACTIVE

Prepared 2026-10-15 for DEV governance review. Every row is a proposal, not legal approval, executable configuration or a date for automatic deletion. All records-series identifiers, approved periods, signatories and disposition authorities remain unverified. An elapsed date creates an appraisal candidate only.

Custodian means the accountable office/role, not whoever has database administrator access. The Provincial Records Officer must map official copies and duplicates to the actual applicable approved schedule. DPO review and provincial/required external records authorities must be recorded before any future execution. The detailed sources/access mechanisms are in [DEV_DATA_LIFECYCLE_AUDIT.md](DEV_DATA_LIFECYCLE_AUDIT.md).

## Clock and hold rules proposed for approval

- Use a recorded meaningful event and latest applicable linked final closure, preserving original event and subsequent reopening/correction history. Administrative browsing, login alone, AI execution, exports, synchronization or updated_at do not automatically extend a service record.
- Calculate calendar months/years in UTC in the synthetic prototype, clamping leap day/month end; elapsed days are 24-hour UTC intervals. The Records Officer must approve local-date/Asia-Manila rules and boundary interpretation before a live implementation.
- Holds override review dates; require scope, authority, owner, start, reason class, periodic review and authorized release. An unknown hold/dependency inventory prevents disposition. Restriction limits processing while preserving evidence.
- Longer required linked-case retention takes precedence over a shorter working-copy proposal. Some records may require permanent archival preservation/transfer rather than destruction.
- These are review targets, not statutory minimums or approval to delete. The five-year recommendation in DEV_RETENTION_RECOMMENDATION.md remains inactive; this matrix adds record-specific dependencies and unknowns.

No personal records were deleted, no timer activated and no proposed state persisted by this milestone.
