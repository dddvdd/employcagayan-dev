EMPLOYCagayan is the Provincial Government of Cagayan PESO employment facilitation platform, built from Quest / Trabaho Caravan. It connects jobseekers, employers and PESO personnel across job fairs and online hiring. Its workflow covers profiles and qualification evidence, public events and QR passes/check-in, approved employers and vacancy publication, applications and candidate recommendations, interviews and recorded outcomes, government employment programs and reporting. Supervisors and staff work within role, event and jurisdiction boundaries.
